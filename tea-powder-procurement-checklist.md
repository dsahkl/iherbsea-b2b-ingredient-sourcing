# Tea Powder Procurement Checklist

Use this checklist when comparing bulk instant tea powder suppliers for RTD tea, milk tea, instant drink powder, foodservice blends and dry beverage mixes.

## 1. Define the Application

- Finished product: RTD tea, milk tea, sachet powder, vending blend, bakery, dessert or foodservice beverage.
- Solubility target: hot soluble, cold soluble, low sediment, fast dispersion or dry blend flow.
- Sensory target: tea body, aroma direction, color depth, bitterness level and aftertaste.
- Processing condition: hot fill, cold fill, dry blending, pasteurization, UHT, pH range or dairy system.
- Label requirement: tea type, carrier status, additive review and market-specific documentation.

## 2. Compare Product Types

| Product type | Review focus | Typical sourcing question |
|---|---|---|
| Instant black tea powder | Tea body, color, dairy compatibility, solubility | Does the sample work in milk tea, RTD tea or dry beverage powder? |
| Instant green tea powder | Color, aroma, sediment, bitterness | Can the grade match the intended beverage or food application? |
| Matcha powder | Color, particle size, taste, origin | Is the grade suitable for beverage, bakery or premium concepts? |
| Oolong or jasmine tea powder | Aroma profile and blend compatibility | Does the sensory profile remain stable in the final matrix? |
| Tea extract powder | Marker compound, assay method, carrier | Is the project focused on beverage flavor or standardized extract specification? |

## 3. Request Documents

- Specification sheet.
- Representative COA with lot number.
- Flow chart or process overview when available.
- Allergen statement.
- Heavy metals and microbiology data.
- Pesticide residue information when required by the market.
- Packing details and shelf-life statement.
- Sample label and sample lot reference.

## 4. Sample Testing

Use the same sample level, liquid temperature, mixing method and holding time across suppliers. Record dry powder appearance and prepared beverage appearance separately.

Review:

- Wetting speed and dispersion.
- Sediment after standing.
- Aroma and taste in the final formula.
- Color before and after processing.
- Mouthfeel and aftertaste.
- Compatibility with milk, sweetener, acid, flavor, stabilizer or minerals.

## 5. Commercial RFQ Details

- Target specification or product type.
- Sample quantity.
- Expected order volume.
- Destination market.
- Packing preference.
- Required documents.
- Timeline for sample, pilot and commercial order.
