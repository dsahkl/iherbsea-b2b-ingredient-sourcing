Please provide available documents:

- Specification sheet.
- Representative COA.
- Flow chart.

- Microbiology data.
- Residual solvent data where relevant.
- Pesticide residue data where relevant.
- Packing information.
- Shelf-life or retest policy.
- Quality questionnaire if available.
