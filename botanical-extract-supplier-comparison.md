# Botanical Extract Supplier Comparison

Use this guide when comparing botanical extract suppliers for supplement, beverage, food, cosmetic and powder blend projects.

## 1. Supplier Qualification

| Review area | Questions to ask |
|---|---|
| Specification depth | Can the supplier provide a clear assay range, method and sample-linked COA? |
| Source control | Can the supplier explain raw material source, plant part and origin? |
| Process clarity | Is the extraction or drying process documented at a level suitable for buyer review? |
| Document readiness | Are specification, COA, allergen statement, flow chart and questionnaire available? |
| Repeat supply | Can the supplier connect the approved sample to a stable commercial lot? |
| Communication | Can technical, quality and commercial questions be answered quickly? |

## 2. Product Review

Compare:

- Extract ratio or marker compound.
- Assay method such as HPLC, UV or titration.
- Appearance, color and odor.
- Particle size and flow.
- Moisture, ash and bulk density when relevant.
- Carrier, excipient and processing aid information.
- Packing format, MOQ, lead time and shipment route.

## 3. Document Checklist

- Specification sheet.
- COA with lot number.
- Method of analysis when available.
- Heavy metals and microbiology data.
- Residual solvent data where relevant.
- Pesticide residue information when required.
- Allergen statement.
- Flow chart.
- Shelf-life or retest policy.
- Quality questionnaire.

## 4. RFQ Scoring Matrix

| Criterion | Score 1 | Score 3 | Score 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specification clarity | Vague | Basic spec | Clear assay and method |
| COA usefulness | Generic | Representative | Lot-linked with method detail |
| Sample support | Unclear | Available | Linked to commercial grade |
| Document package | Limited | Basic | Complete buyer-ready package |
| Commercial fit | Unknown | MOQ/lead time provided | MOQ, lead time, packing and repeat supply clear |
