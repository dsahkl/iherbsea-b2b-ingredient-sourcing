Organic Cranberry Extract COA Review

Before sourcing organic cranberry extract, align the specification, sample lot and COA fields with the buyer's internal quality brief.

Document Checks

For botanical fruit extracts, buyers should confirm the ingredient identity, extraction direction, carrier status and batch document package. Organic status should only be used when the supplier can provide documents that match the buyer's market requirement.

COA AreaWhat to Confirm
IdentityProduct name, botanical source, plant part, extract ratio or marker direction where relevant.
Batch linkLot number, sample reference, release date and whether the COA represents the reviewed grade.
Quality fieldsAppearance, moisture, microbiology, heavy metals and other buyer-required fields.
Commercial supportPacking, MOQ, lead time, shelf-life note and repeat supply plan.
