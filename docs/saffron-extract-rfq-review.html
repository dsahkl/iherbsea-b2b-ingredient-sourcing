RFQ Review Points

Saffron extract comparison should keep product identity, documents, sample lots and commercial assumptions in one record.

RFQ pointBuyer check
IdentityConfirm ingredient name, source description, appearance and any agreed marker language.
Quality fileRequest COA, specification sheet, microbiology, heavy metals and packing information.
Sample planDefine sample quantity, test format, internal deadline and response owner.
Commercial fitCompare MOQ, lead time, price basis and destination requirements.
