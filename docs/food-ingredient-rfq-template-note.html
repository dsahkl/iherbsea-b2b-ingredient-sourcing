Food Ingredient RFQ Template Note

Use a structured RFQ before asking suppliers for quotation so technical and commercial details stay aligned.

RFQ Preparation Areas

Food ingredient RFQs should connect the target ingredient with application, specification, sample request, document review and commercial planning. A complete RFQ reduces back-and-forth during supplier comparison.

RFQ areaBuyer detail
SpecificationIngredient name, assay or grade, physical requirements and application needs.
DocumentationCOA, specification sheet, allergen statement, packing and quality files.
SampleSample quantity, number of options, intended test method and timeline.
CommercialMOQ expectation, forecast range, packing, destination and lead-time question.
