Document Review Areas

A consistent COA review keeps the quality team and purchasing team aligned before moving a powder sample into application testing.

Document areaWhat to check
IdentityConfirm product name, botanical source where provided, carrier and physical description.
Quality fieldsReview moisture, ash, microbiology, heavy metals and any agreed specification limits.
Sample linkMatch sample label, lot number and document date before internal testing.
Supplier responseRecord whether the supplier can provide current specification and packing details.
