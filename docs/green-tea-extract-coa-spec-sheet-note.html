Green Tea Extract COA Spec Sheet Note

Compare green tea extract documents by marker, assay method, sample lot and specification wording before supplier selection.

Document Comparison

Green tea extract sourcing often involves several marker and method options. Buyers should compare the specification sheet and COA together rather than reviewing either document alone.

Document fieldBuyer check
MarkerConfirm the target marker or extract ratio used in the project brief.
Assay methodConfirm whether the supplier states HPLC, UV or another agreed method.
Sample lotConnect sample label, COA lot number and commercial supply discussion.
Application fitConfirm powder behavior, carrier status and packing details for the intended format.
