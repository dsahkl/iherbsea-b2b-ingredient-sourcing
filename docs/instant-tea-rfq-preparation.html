Instant Tea RFQ Preparation

Prepare instant tea powder RFQs with application, solubility and document details before asking suppliers for price.

RFQ Checklist

Instant tea powder can be used across hot drinks, iced drinks, milk tea, RTD concepts and dry mix formulas. A good RFQ tells the supplier how the material will be tested and what evidence the buyer needs for approval.

RFQ SectionDetails to Provide
Tea baseBlack, green, oolong, jasmine or other direction; state whether instant solubility is required.
ApplicationHot beverage, cold beverage, milk tea, RTD, powder blend or food application.
Sample testWater temperature, dilution, mixing time, rest time and evaluation points.
DocumentsSpecification, COA, packing, shelf-life note and any buyer-specific quality fields.
