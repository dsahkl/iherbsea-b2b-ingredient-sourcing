Green Tea Extract Assay Method Review

Compare green tea extract suppliers by marker method, COA wording and sample evidence before price negotiation.

Buyer Review Points

FieldWhat to Check
Assay modelConfirm whether the offer is based on tea polyphenols, catechins, EGCG or another marker.
MethodAsk whether HPLC, UV or another validated method is used, and keep method language consistent across suppliers.
COA matchCheck lot number, marker value, method, specification range, microbiology and contaminant fields.
Application fitRecord color, taste, solubility and finished formula behavior when the extract is used in powder or beverage systems.
