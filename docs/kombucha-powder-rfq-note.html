RFQ Fields

Kombucha powder sourcing becomes cleaner when specification, sample, packing and commercial fields are captured together.

RFQ areaBuyer detail
SpecificationConfirm appearance, carrier, moisture, microbiology and any flavor direction.
Document packageRequest COA, spec sheet, allergen statement and packing information.
SampleTrack sample lot, quantity and test schedule.
CommercialCompare MOQ, lead time, quote validity and destination market.
