Plant Extract Supplier Shortlist Criteria

Shortlist plant extract suppliers by document clarity, sample support and commercial fit, not by broad ingredient names alone.

Comparison Criteria

Plant extract sourcing should start with the buyer's target application and internal quality requirements. A useful shortlist connects specification fields with sample behavior and supplier response quality.

CriterionPractical Signal
Specification clarityBotanical source, plant part, extract ratio or marker, assay method and physical properties are stated clearly.
COA readinessRepresentative or current COA can be reviewed with lot number, limits and test results.
Sample supportSupplier can match sample size, application notes and document package before quotation.
Commercial fitMOQ, packing, lead time, repeat order planning and destination needs are discussed early.
