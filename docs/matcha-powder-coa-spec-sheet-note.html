Matcha Review Fields

Matcha powder sourcing depends on grade, color, particle size, application and document clarity. Buyers should define these fields before comparing supplier samples.

FieldWhat to compare
Grade and applicationDefine beverage, latte, bakery, dry mix or food-service use before sample review.
Physical qualityReview color, particle size, flow, aroma and prepared application behavior.
COA and specificationCompare lot number, limits, test results, packing and shelf-life note.
RFQ readinessPrepare sample quantity, target order range, packing and destination details.
