Supplier Document Areas

A clean document package helps buyers separate product fit from missing information during supplier review.

AreaReview point
SpecificationCheck product name, extraction ratio or marker language where available, appearance and carrier.
COAReview identity, assay method, heavy metals, microbiology, moisture and residual solvent fields.
SampleMatch sample lot, label and document date before internal formulation work.
QuotationAsk for MOQ, lead time, packing and document turnaround before supplier comparison.
