Spice Powder Specification Notes

Bulk spice powder sourcing should connect sensory needs, particle size, quality data and packing before price comparison.

Specification Points

Spice powders are often selected for flavor, color and processing behavior. Buyers should avoid comparing only by name, because mesh size, microbial control, country of origin and storage condition can change application fit.

AreaWhat to Compare
IdentityBotanical or common name, plant part, origin direction and grade description.
Physical fitMesh size, color, aroma, flow, dust level and caking tendency under storage.
Quality dataMicrobiology, moisture, foreign matter and heavy metals where required by buyer policy.
PackingBag size, carton or drum option, pallet plan, label details and shelf-life assumption.
