# Fruit Powder Supplier Checklist

Use this checklist when comparing fruit powder suppliers for beverage, bakery, nutrition powder, dry mix, confectionery and foodservice applications.

## 1. Application Fit

- Finished product: beverage powder, smoothie mix, bakery, snack, confectionery, dairy, dessert or nutrition blend.
- Processing route: spray drying, freeze drying, drum drying, grinding or extraction.
- Sensory target: fruit aroma, acidity, sweetness, color and mouthfeel.
- Functional requirement: dry blend flow, instant dispersion, color stability or flavor impact.
- Label requirement: fruit source, carrier, allergen status and market-specific documentation.

## 2. Specification Review

| Item | What to compare |
|---|---|
| Fruit source | Species, variety or approved raw material source when relevant. |
| Powder type | Juice powder, whole fruit powder, extract powder or spray-dried powder. |
| Carrier status | Maltodextrin, gum arabic or carrier-free claim where applicable. |
| Particle size | Mesh size, flow and dust level for blending or filling. |
| Moisture | Storage and caking behavior. |
| Color and odor | Batch consistency and finished product impact. |
| Microbiology | Food application and destination market requirements. |
| Heavy metals and pesticide residues | Buyer policy and market-specific review. |

## 3. Sample Testing

Test samples under the same conditions:

- Dry powder appearance and flow.
- Dispersion in water or final formula.
- Color before and after processing.
- Aroma and flavor contribution.
- Sediment or insoluble matter after standing.
- Compatibility with sweetener, acid, dairy, minerals or other botanicals.

## 4. Documents to Request

- Specification sheet.
- Representative COA.
- Allergen statement.
- Flow chart or process description.
- Packing information.
- Shelf-life or retest policy.
- Heavy metals and microbiology data.
- Pesticide residue statement when needed.
