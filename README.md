# B2B Ingredient Sourcing Resources

Practical checklists for food, beverage, supplement and botanical ingredient buyers who need to compare suppliers, specifications, COA documents, samples and RFQ details before commercial purchasing.

This repository is maintained as a lightweight public resource for procurement, quality and R&D teams. It does not provide medical, dosage or consumer health recommendations.

## Resource Guides

- [Tea Powder Procurement Checklist](tea-powder-procurement-checklist.md) - compare instant tea powder suppliers by application, solubility, sensory profile, documents, sample testing and quotation details.
- [Plant Extract COA Review Checklist](plant-extract-coa-review-checklist.md) - review assay method, identity, heavy metals, microbiology, residual solvents, sample lots and document readiness.
- [Fruit Powder Supplier Checklist](fruit-powder-supplier-checklist.md) - compare fruit powder suppliers for beverage, bakery, nutrition powder and food applications.
- [Spice Powder Supplier Checklist](spice-powder-supplier-checklist.md) - review bulk spice powder specification, color, aroma, mesh size, microbiology and packing details.
- [Botanical Extract Supplier Comparison](botanical-extract-supplier-comparison.md) - compare botanical extract suppliers by assay, document readiness, sample support and commercial fit.
- [Supplier RFQ Template](supplier-rfq-template.md) - a practical template for requesting ingredient specifications, COA, samples, packing information, MOQ and lead time.
