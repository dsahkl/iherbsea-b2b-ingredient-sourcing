# Plant Extract COA Review Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing botanical extract powders, fruit extracts, mushroom extracts, flavonoid ingredients and other bulk plant-derived materials.

## 1. Identity and Specification

- Product name and botanical source.
- Plant part or raw material source where applicable.
- Extract ratio or marker compound.
- Assay range and assay method.
- Carrier, excipient or processing aid information.
- Appearance, color, odor and particle size.
- Packaging format and storage condition.

## 2. COA Review Points

| COA item | What to check |
|---|---|
| Lot number | The COA should match the sample or commercial lot under review. |
| Assay method | Confirm HPLC, UV, titration or other agreed method. |
| Assay result | Compare with specification range and calculation basis. |
| Moisture and ash | Review against storage and formulation requirements. |
| Heavy metals | Compare with destination market and internal limits. |
| Microbiology | Review total plate count, yeast and mold, E. coli, Salmonella and other required items. |
| Residual solvents | Required for many extract projects depending on process and market. |
| Pesticide residues | Request when relevant to market or buyer policy. |
| Retest or shelf-life | Confirm date format and storage assumptions. |

## 3. Supplier Questions

- Can the supplier provide a current specification sheet?
- Is the sample linked to a representative production lot?
- Can the supplier confirm origin, process route and packing size?
- Are allergen, flow chart and quality questionnaire documents available?
- Can the supplier support repeat supply under the same specification?
- What change-control notice is provided if specification, source or process changes?

## 4. Sample Review

Record the same points for each sample:

- Appearance, color and odor.
- Particle size and flow.
- Bulk density when important for filling or blending.
- Solubility, dispersion or suspension behavior.
- Taste impact when used in beverage or food formats.
- Compatibility with capsule, tablet, powder blend or beverage systems.
