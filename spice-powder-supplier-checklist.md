# Spice Powder Supplier Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing bulk spice powder suppliers for seasoning blends, sauces, instant foods, savory snacks, meat alternatives, foodservice products and dry mixes.

## 1. Define the Spice Powder Requirement

- Product type: white pepper powder, black pepper powder, turmeric powder, cinnamon powder, clove powder, cumin powder or custom spice blend.
- Application: seasoning, sauce, dry mix, instant food, savory snack, bakery or ready meal.
- Sensory target: aroma intensity, pungency, color, aftertaste and heat level.
- Processing requirement: heat exposure, blending, sieving, metal detection and packing.
- Label requirement: origin, irradiation status, steam treatment, allergen statement and market-specific documents.

## 2. Specification Review

| Item | What to compare |
|---|---|
| Botanical identity | Species, plant part and origin where relevant. |
| Mesh size | Flow, mouthfeel, suspension and dust behavior. |
| Color | Visual consistency and finished product impact. |
| Aroma | Freshness, volatility and application fit. |
| Moisture | Caking risk and storage stability. |
| Microbiology | Food safety review and process validation. |
| Foreign matter | Cleaning, sorting and supplier quality control. |
| Packing | Bag size, liner, carton or drum format, pallet plan. |

## 3. COA and Quality Review

Request:

- Specification sheet.
- Representative COA.
- Microbiology data.
- Heavy metals data.
- Pesticide residue information when required.
- Allergen statement.
- Flow chart or process overview.
- Irradiation or steam treatment statement where relevant.
- Packing and shelf-life information.

## 4. RFQ Details

Include:

- Target spice powder and mesh size.
- Application and sensory target.
- Sample quantity.
- Expected order volume.
- Destination market.
- Packing preference.
- Document requirements.
